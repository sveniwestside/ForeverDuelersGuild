ForeverDuel 0.4.1 - Experimental beta / testing release
Target client: WoW: Forever 1.60.1 (interface 16001)

- Added direct addon discovery through target, focus, group members, and player nameplates. Queries and replies use addon whispers when the shared discovery channel is unavailable or does not deliver messages.
- Channel join support is now optional. Direct discovery remains available without it, with paced requests, bounded retries, and no response loops.
- Replaced cycling filter buttons with dropdown menus for class, rating window, sorting, and rated eligibility.
- Removed Death Knight, Monk, Demon Hunter, and Evoker from zone filters and discovery profiles. The nine Classic classes remain.
- Improved empty-list guidance and /duelrating status whisper diagnostics.

Install 0.4.1 on both clients for direct discovery. If the list stays empty, target the other character once or enable player nameplates. The addon cannot enumerate arbitrary nearby players without a native unit or a received announcement.

Ratings, saved data, and rated-duel protocol are unchanged from 0.4.0. Earlier 0.4.0 clients can still exchange channel announcements and rated-duel messages, but do not answer the new direct-discovery queries.

Automated Lua regressions cover the changes. Native discovery and dropdown rendering still require a focused two-client in-game check; the exact cause of the reported channel delivery failure has not been confirmed from client diagnostics.
