RATING & REPORTS

What counts here.

Every rating has a duel history behind it.

01

The duel is recorded in game.

ForeverDuelersGuild records your duels and maintains your local add-on rating. Max-level and leveling duels stay separate.

02

Both participants upload their history.

Two matching reports confirm the result for the website. Conflicting reports do not count towards the ladder.

03

The ladder recalculates its ratings.

Everyone starts at 1,500. Leveling duels count with a maximum gap of five levels. Website ratings are calculated independently of your local add-on rating.

i

About the data: Matching uploads provide community confirmation, not proof from the WoW server. Blizzard character data enriches available profiles; it does not confirm duel results.
