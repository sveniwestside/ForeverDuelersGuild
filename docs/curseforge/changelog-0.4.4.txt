ForeverDuel 0.4.4 - Experimental beta / testing release
Target client: WoW: Forever 1.60.1 (interface 16001)

FIXED IN 0.4.4

- Retry incoming player identification when the challenger was not exposed as a native unit at the initial request. The ordinary WoW dialog remains available, with a chat hint to target the challenger.
- Retry every half-second only while the same native request popup remains visibly open and within the original 50-second pending limit. Missing popup API support preserves ordinary play.
- Stop recovery after native acceptance, decline/cancellation, countdown, completion, expiry, replacement, combat, world transitions, or errors. Observed identity ambiguity remains terminal for that request.
- Show incoming-request recovery in /duelrating status even with debug logging disabled. Debug remains optional and does not control consent, discovery, or rating.
- Preserve rating rules, saved data, automatic discovery, schema 2, and rated protocol 2. Both players must still explicitly consent.

UPGRADING

Back up your character's SavedVariables/ForeverDuel.lua before installing. Update both players to 0.4.4. When upgrading from 0.4.3, /reload on both clients loads the change; this update adds no manifest module. Fully restart clients when upgrading from before 0.4.3 to load its Roster.lua module.

Updating from 0.4.0 through 0.4.3 preserves both rating modes and history. The rated protocol remains compatible with 0.4.0.

TEST STATUS

33 Lua files compile; 14 suites and 4,102 assertions pass under Lua 5.1. The delayed incoming-identity regression failed before the fix. Simulated clients complete a rated duel with debug switched off after having been enabled, including a receiver whose challenger identity appears after the initial request. Guards cover ordinary acceptance/decline, stale callbacks, expiry, lifecycle changes, and ambiguity.
