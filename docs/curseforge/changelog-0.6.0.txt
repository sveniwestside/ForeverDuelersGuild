ForeverDuelersGuild 0.6.0 - Beta
Target client: WoW: Forever 1.60.1 (interface 16001)

The first update since 0.4.5, and a big one. All details are on the project page.

Before you update
- Both players need 0.6.0 for rated duels. Against 0.4.5 the duel stays ordinary.
- Restart the game completely after installing. /reload is not enough.
- Your ratings and history carry over. Back up SavedVariables/ForeverDuel.lua first: once 0.6.0 has recorded a rated duel, 0.4.5 can no longer load it.

New
- Find addon players on the whole realm: Horde characters join the in-game community ForeverDuelersGuild with /duelrating community join (no Alliance community yet).
- Opt-in queue (/duelrating queue) that pairs you with players at meeting places you have both saved.
- Rated duels work alongside Blizzard's normal duel popup, without the old 12-second limit, and results are more reliable.
- German translation, and /duelrating status, diagnose, errors and ping for bug reports.
